We build tools for making forecasts, comparing the reasoning behind them, and checking what happened next. Our flagship product, **iPulse AI**, is an Open Agentic Investment Research Platform. **Forecast Library** is public infrastructure for preserving and verifying forecasts from people, statistical models, and AI.

### Models, methods, and evaluation

Our [September 2026 Batch 7 release](https://ipulseai.com/blog/batch-7-ipulse-ai-engine-v7) used **Google Gemini 3.8 Flash**, **Anthropic Claude Opus 5**, and **OpenAI GPT-6 Astra**. It used 14 analyst configurations per asset, including a matched research framework across model families so readers can compare how different models weigh the same assignment. The individual reports sit alongside a synthesized consensus, with visible disagreements and risks.

## Forecast Library

Publish or inspect dated forecasts and the evidence behind them. Each forecast can have a portable receipt with provenance and an integrity check, plus an optional blockchain attestation. A verified receipt helps establish the integrity of a record. Forecast accuracy is a separate question.

[Browse forecasts](https://forecastlibrary.com/forecasts) · [Forecaster profiles](https://forecastlibrary.com/forecasters) · [Receipt specification](https://forecastlibrary.com/standards/open-forecast-receipt/v0-1) · [Try the integrity test](https://forecastlibrary.com/integrity-test) · [Open Forecast Receipt source code](https://github.com/TheFutureEdge/open-forecast-receipt)
