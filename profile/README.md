# Future Edge Group

We build tools for making forecasts and examining how they were made. Our work spans agentic investment research, forecast evaluation, and open standards for recording and verifying forecasts.

[Future Edge Group](https://ftredge.com/) · [iPulse AI](https://ipulseai.com/) · [Forecast Library](https://forecastlibrary.com/) · [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/company/future-edge-group)

## iPulse AI

iPulse AI is an **Open Agentic Investment Research Platform** for stocks, crypto, commodities, currencies, and indices. Independent AI analysts build forecasts from market data and research evidence. iPulse AI publishes the individual views alongside a consensus rating, dated price targets, and the risks that could change the forecast.

## Forecast Library

Forecast Library explores how to document, preserve, and evaluate forecasts across domains. Its [Open Forecast Receipt specification](https://forecastlibrary.com/standards/open-forecast-receipt/v0-1) describes how to record forecast provenance and verify the integrity of a published record.

[Visit Forecast Library](https://forecastlibrary.com/) · [Open Forecast Receipt on GitHub](https://github.com/TheFutureEdge/open-forecast-receipt)

## Public research and open source

- [When Models Judge Models](https://github.com/TheFutureEdge/research-when-models-judge-models) studies how source identity and disagreement shape multi-agent forecasts.
- [When AI Advisors Agree](https://github.com/TheFutureEdge/research-when-ai-advisors-agree) tests what agreement reveals about forecast risk and reliability.
- [When Is Fresh AI Stock Analysis Worth It?](https://github.com/TheFutureEdge/research-when-fresh-ai-stock-analysis-is-worth-it) studies when to refresh research and when to reuse existing forecasts.
- [Historical consensus snapshots](https://github.com/TheFutureEdge/ipulse-ai-historical-consensus-snapshots) preserve dated iPulse AI consensus records for historical review.
- [GlossAnchor](https://github.com/TheFutureEdge/glossanchor) and [Open Forecast Receipt](https://github.com/TheFutureEdge/open-forecast-receipt) are public projects in the Future Edge Group portfolio.
